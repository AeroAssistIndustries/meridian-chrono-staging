    How every watch is authenticated

    Nothing is offered until it has passed all four checks.

    Provenance

    Serial and reference matched to the warranty card and dealer records, and checked against stolen-watch registries.

    Movement

    Caseback opened, calibre identified and inspected, then timed for rate and amplitude.

    Case and bracelet

    Dial, hands, bezel, crystal and bracelet compared to factory specification for the year.

    Final review

    A second specialist signs off before the watch is photographed, listed or shipped.

    About us

    A collector's network, opened to clients.

    Meridian Chrono Group is a private watch acquisition firm. We find, authenticate and deliver the watches our clients ask for, from current steel sports models to pieces that rarely change hands.

    The firm grew out of founder Rushik Dave's own collecting. Over more than eight years of buying from authorized dealers around the world, he built the kind of relationships that decide who is offered the most requested watches. Meridian puts that access to work for its clients.

    A good watch outlasts trends, markets and often its first owner. We try to run the firm the same way: slowly, carefully and with the long view in mind.

    We work from the client's brief. You tell us what you want, and we source it, verify it and deliver it. Because we source on your behalf, the advice you get is about the right watch for you.

    Nearly every client comes to us by referral, and many have bought with us for years. We keep our client list and their purchases private.

    We are deliberately small. A watch is one of the few things a person buys intending to keep for decades and then hand on, and a purchase like that deserves a dealer who knows your name, remembers what you already own and will tell you when to wait.

    How Meridian began

    1. The collection

      Rushik bought his first serious watch years before Meridian had a name. One became several, and the hunt for each taught him how the market really works: who gets the call, and why.

      It also taught him what the brochures leave out. The watch you chase for a year can be the one you sell in a month, and the quiet reference nobody mentions can be the one you never take off.

    2. The first requests

      Friends began asking him to find the watches they could not get. He made the calls, checked each piece himself and handed it over. Word travelled.

      Those early favours set the standard we still hold. If he would not have bought the watch himself, at that price and in that condition, he did not pass it on.

    3. The firm

      Meridian Chrono Group was set up to do that properly, with a team to authenticate, a process that protects the client and the same network behind it.

      The name comes from the meridian, the line from which the world's time is measured. It is a reminder that in this trade everything is judged against a reference point, and ours is the client's interest.

    4. Today

      We source and sell for private clients in the US, India, Europe and the Middle East. Most new clients still arrive through an introduction.

      We have grown, but not by much, and on purpose. Reputation in this business is built one watch at a time and can be lost with one, so we would rather do fewer deals and do each of them properly.

    How we work

    The brief comes first

    Every search starts with what you want: the reference, the dial, the budget and the timing. We ask a lot of questions at the start, about how you live, what you already wear and what the watch is for, because the right answer for a daily watch is rarely the right answer for a milestone.

    Relationships over listings

    Our sourcing runs through dealers and collectors we know, built over years of buying. The best watches rarely appear on a public listing. They change hands between people who trust each other, and trust in this trade is earned by paying on time, describing honestly and keeping your word when it costs you.

    Verified before offered

    Each watch passes provenance, movement and case checks and a second review. We would rather lose a sale than sell a doubt. If a piece raises a question we cannot answer, it goes back, however good the price looked.

    Private by default

    Conversations, purchases and collections stay between you and us. We do not post client purchases, name the people we work with or discuss what anyone owns. What is on your wrist is your business.

    What collecting has taught us

    Lessons learned at our own expense, so that our clients do not have to pay for them twice.

    1. Buy the watch, not the hype.

      Waiting lists and headlines move on. The watch stays on your wrist. If you would not want it at half the attention, it is the attention you want.

    2. Condition is the one thing you cannot add later.

      You can find a box, trace papers and service a movement. You cannot unpolish a case or restore an original dial. Pay for honest condition before anything else.

    3. The best price is rarely the lowest one.

      A cheap watch with a question mark costs more in the end than a fair one with a clean history. The saving disappears the day you try to sell it.

    4. Patience is a collector's sharpest tool.

      The right example comes around again. The wrong one bought in a hurry sits in a drawer. We will tell you when to wait, even though waiting earns us nothing.

    5. Wear your watches.

      A watch kept in a safe is an asset. A watch on the wrist is a companion, and its marks become your history. The ones people regret selling are always the ones they wore.

    6. Buy the seller before you buy the watch.

      Every watch comes with a story, and the story is only as good as the person telling it. Ask who stands behind it and whether they will take your call in five years.

    7. Keep everything.

      The box, the card, the spare links, the service receipts. They take up a shelf and cost nothing to keep, and together they can decide what the watch is worth.

    8. A watch marks time twice.

      Once on the dial, and once in your life. The pieces that matter most are tied to a moment: a first success, a child, a promise kept. Choose with that in mind and you rarely choose badly.

        Questions

        How does private sourcing work?

        Send us a brief with the reference and anything that matters to you. We search our dealer and collector network and come back with real pieces, photos and a price. You only commit once you have approved a specific watch.

        Do you buy watches?

        Yes. We buy outright and take trade-ins toward any watch we sell or source. Use the sell form for an offer.

        Is every watch authenticated?

        Every watch passes provenance, movement, case and bracelet checks and a second specialist's review before it is offered.

        How do I pay?

        Most clients pay by bank wire. A specialist confirms the payment options and sends an invoice once you reserve a watch.

        Can I make an offer?

        Yes. Open any priced watch and choose Make an offer. A specialist reviews it and replies.

        What does "box and papers" mean?

        The original presentation box and the warranty card or certificate issued when the watch was first sold. Together with the accessories they make a full set.

        How do you describe condition?

        Unworn means the watch has not been worn since it was first sold. Excellent means only light signs of wear. Very good means visible light wear on a fully working watch.

        How do I reach you?

        Call or text +1 (520) 728-4553, email info@meridianchrono.com, or message @meridianchronogroup on Instagram. We are based in Arizona and work with clients worldwide.

        Do you deliver outside the US?

        We work with clients in the US, India, Europe and the Middle East. Delivery, insurance and timing are agreed with you and stated on your invoice.

        Which currency are prices in?

        Prices and invoices are in US dollars. If you switch the site to another currency, the figures shown are approximate conversions at the day's rate.

        How long does sourcing take?

        It depends on the reference. We tell you what to expect at the start and keep you updated until we have a piece to show you.

        Can I try a watch on?

        Every watch page has a virtual try-on that shows the watch true to scale on your wrist size, or on a photo of your own wrist. For clients nearby we can arrange a private viewing.

        Can I see a watch before buying?

        We send detailed photos and video of the exact piece on request, and can arrange a private viewing for clients nearby.
